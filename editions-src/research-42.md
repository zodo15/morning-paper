## 1. FAA ground stop - the FAA's own account (LEAD, fresh Sunday)
Owning: FAA "Nationwide Ground Stop: Questions and Answers", dated Sunday Nov 15 - https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/nationwide-ground-stop-questions-and-answers (read in full)
- Friday Nov 13 ~3:30pm ET technical problem -> nationwide ground stop; stable and resumed 8:15pm same day.
- Cause: legacy equipment unstable when data flooded the FAA FTI network at 1,000 connection attempts every 4-5 seconds, disrupting aircraft comms + radar feeds.
- TWO potential locations: a radar site in Amarillo, Texas AND a radio communications site in El Dorado, Arkansas. L3Harris (FAA's current network service provider) had been working at El Dorado, including deploying a new router. L3Harris blocked both locations -> network stabilized. Broadcasting function disabled on ALL new routers as precaution.
- Instability at 17 of 22 ARTCCs, spread to several TRACONs. ~3,500 aircraft landed safely.
- "Based on all available evidence, we have no indication that this was a cyberattack or an outside nefarious actor."
- NOT the modernization: "due to the legacy TDM system"; none of the towers upgraded under the Working Families Tax Cuts down payment went down. SMART system unrelated (data aggregator, doesn't feed ATC).
- Root cause still under investigation by FAA + L3Harris.
Sunday recovery state (ABC News live blog, Nov 15): 17,500+ flights canceled Fri+Sat combined; only 30 Monday flights canceled as of late Sunday morning; Duffy on This Week: "we're going to see things come back to normal today, but probably fully normal tomorrow"; the specific Arkansas router is in L3Harris's Florida lab for investigation; routers' data-broadcast ability turned off. Bedford Saturday: ground stop was "the result of faulty equipment"; "zero evidence of anything nefarious, and zero evidence of anything AI-related". Reuters (Nov 14): Sat 6,400+ canceled / 8,000+ delayed as of 9:35pm ET; Fri 9,200 canceled / 7,500 delayed; longest nationwide halt since 9/11; White House Situation Room meeting Friday; Duffy seeks $50bn for ATC upgrades. https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/business/aerospace-defense/thousands-us-flights-canceled-delayed-air-travel-system-recovers-shutdown-2026-11-14/ ; ABC live: https://abcnews.com/US/live-updates/faa-ground-stop-live-updates-hundreds-flights-canceled/?entryId=138160582&id=138125140
- Bedford's "faulty equipment" vs FAA Q&A "investigation continues": keep cause OPEN (router in lab; two sites; root cause not final).
