# LR53 research - Asian Paints (the company that outlasted its founders)
Compiled 04:35 IST. 8 sources read fully + current-state checks. Historical spine exempt from the 7-day rule; current-state items dated.

## Why this one
India's largest paint company for 59 unbroken years (leader since 1967 per the company itself) - built by four friends in a wartime garage, taken to #1 in 25 years, and engineered as an institution that survived the 1997 exit of its own founding visionary's family. Never covered (LR33-52 checked: Parle/biscuit 33, bhujia 34, app 35, chemist 36, tyres 37, pool-table cloud 38, booth 39, ARM 40, aviation network 41, Anthropic 42, procurement 43, TSMC 44, ASML 45, NSE 46, BSE 47, zero-billion bets 48, Infosys 49, Maruti 50, Dixon 51, APL Apollo 52).
