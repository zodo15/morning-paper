## Spine (facts, with source numbers)
- Origin: Tata Computer Centre, a shared computing utility for group companies, set up at Nirmal Building, Nariman Point; went under-used; Bombay House remodelled it as TCS (1). Founding year differs: 1966 centre (1), "established 1967 as Tata Computer Centre" (4), TCS 1968 (1,3,6). Print "1968" with "began as the Tata Computer Centre"; do not print 1966/1967.
- Founders: three MIT management graduates (Lalit Kanodia, Nitin Patel, Ashok Malhotra) wrote the data-centre project report; Yashpal Sahni a founding member; Kohli succeeded Kanodia in 1969 (4). Kohli, general manager at Tata Electric (digital load dispatch at Trombay), moved late 1969 "for a year" (4,5,2). JRD Tata lobbied for him (2).
- Group companies were reluctant to hand over data work, so TCS had to find outside clients (4). First external customer 1971 Bombay Telephones, directory digitisation under a World Bank-funded project (4). First overseas assignment 1971, an electric company in Iran (3). Early work: bank reconciliation, payroll, accounting (3,2). First mainframe: a never-used ICL 1903 bought cheap from a government insurer after a union blocked installation (5).
- Market was IBM and ICL, importing used machines; CAG audit found invoicing malpractice; government handed it to the new Department of Electronics (4). Kohli worked with Burroughs; visited US at least nine times 1972-74 (4).
- Eureka: no Burroughs machine in India (import would take two years, per Kohli). Engineers built a software filter on the ICL machine; code was shipped to the US (no data links), tested on Burroughs machines, worked (4). Tata timeline: 1973 Burroughs partnership; first offshore delivery for a Detroit hospital (3). 1974 first full software life-cycle project: financial accounting package for UK building societies (1,3). 1976 crossed $1 million export revenue; top exporter award (1,3).
- Import bargain: government allowed a Burroughs mainframe if TCS exported twice its value over five years; machine cost $340,000, with tariffs TCS paid over twice that; TCS met the export promise (5).
- 1975 Kohli speech at Computer Society of India: "If we miss this opportunity, those who follow us will not forgive us for tardiness and negligence" (4; shortened version in 2). 
- 1978 parted ways with Burroughs, independent (3); Tata Burroughs formed separately (4). Ramadorai: cutting the cord "the best thing that happened to us" (9). 1979 New York office on Park Avenue; IGIC contract with 24-hour delivery cycle (3). 1981 TRDDC Pune, India's first software research centre (3). 1989 SEGA securities-clearing deal, $10m per (1) / world's first real-time securities clearing (3). 1992 core platform for new NSE (3). Y2K: ~700m lines fixed (1,2).
- Leadership: Kohli to Ramadorai as CEO: 1996 (3,9) vs "stepping down after 22 years" listed under 1989-98 (1) vs "retired in 1999" (2). Print "1996" (two sources, timeline). Ramadorai led 13 years -> N Chandrasekaran CEO 2009; Chandrasekaran Tata Sons chairman 2017; Gopinathan CEO 2017; K Krithivasan CEO 2023 (3).
- 2002 $100m GE Medical (largest by an Indian firm then); 2003 crossed $1bn revenue (first Indian IT services firm) (1,3). 
- IPO Aug 2004 (6): Rs 850/share, Rs 5,420 cr ($1.17bn), India's largest ever, base offer 10.3x subscribed (overall 7.7x), 13.3% stake, valued $8.78bn; Ratan Tata and Ramadorai quotes; previous record Reliance Petroleum Rs 2,172 cr. Ramadorai: took Tata Sons "a sensitive topic" to list (9). Tata timeline: "third-most valuable company immediately" (3) vs Reuters "India's fourth-biggest" at pricing (6): different moments; print Reuters at pricing only.
- 2012 $10bn revenue (3). 2018 first Indian listed company over $100bn market cap (2,3).
- Today (7,10): Q2 FY27 revenue $7,642m (+0.2% QoQ, +2.4% YoY USD); Rs 73,188 cr (+11.2% YoY rupee); operating margin 24.0%; net margin 19.0%; net profit Rs 13,884 cr (company/ET Now; BusinessToday says 13,934: use 13,884); annualised AI revenue $3.1bn, >10% of revenue; TCV $9.6bn; headcount 598,056; attrition 13.3%; dividend Rs 12; Porsche AG 5-year partnership + MHP acquisition (subject to approvals); Best Buy GCC transition.
- Tata Sons link (8): dividends from TCS Rs 28,291 cr FY26 vs record Rs 32,184 cr FY25 (-12%); no buyback either year; cumulative Rs 1.81 lakh cr FY20-FY26; Air India, Tata Digital, Tata Electronics combined FY26 loss Rs 28,823 cr (Moneycontrol calculation). Tata Sons standalone profit Rs 31,961 cr.
