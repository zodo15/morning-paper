## Sources read fully (research leg)
1. Company our-story (owning): born 1986; HQ Delhi NCR; 11 manufacturing units, 5.0 Mn MTPA (+0.4 Mn UAE facility, up to 300x300x12.7mm sections); 3-tier distribution, 800+ dedicated dealers, 50K retailers, 200K fabricators, 29 branch offices, 5,000+ product varieties, 20 iconic global customers. Clients shown: L&T, Jal Jeevan Mission, Indian Railways, Airports Authority of India, Tata Projects, NCC, NBCC. https://aplapollo.com/about/our-story
2. CNBC-TV18 profile/deal analysis (Nov 24, upd Nov 25): the full deal anatomy + market verdict + build-story spine. 1986: Sudesh Gupta incorporates Bihar Tubes as a private firm in Sikandrabad, UP. Sanjay Gupta joins the board 2003; rebrands to APL Apollo Tubes 2010; strategy shift from selling unbranded pipes by weight to branded building materials - colour-coded packaging, retail/distributor expansion, consumer-facing ads, direct architect/engineer engagement; sub-brands Apollo Structural, Apollo Galv, Apollo Tricoat, Apollo Pipes; ~55% of India's structural steel tube market; plants in Murbad, Hosur, Raipur, Hyderabad etc + 0.3MT Dubai exporting to 20+ countries. Deal: Tata Steel buys 26.38% from S Gupta Holding Pvt Ltd for ~Rs 15,510 cr at Rs 2,100/share (9.91% block deal Monday + tranches); open offer for 26% more at Rs 2,301.40; APL Apollo buys Tata's tubes + colour-coated businesses for up to Rs 28,000 cr; Sanjay Gupta's stake falls from ~26.6% to under 2%. Market: -22% Monday (biggest one-day drop in 20 years), another -20% intraday Wednesday before partial recovery; SG Mart and SG Finserve -20% each Wednesday; 4+ analyst downgrades, ICICI Securities and Axis Capital cut targets >=40%; debt-funded leverage concern; the stock historically traded at building-materials/FMCG multiples on Gupta's execution record. Samir Arora (Helios Capital) on-record: "Can a promoter commit his company to a significant transaction while simultaneously selling all his shares at the same time? This way promoter can get a better price for himself in return for committing his company to an unfavourable transaction." https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/apl-apolli-tubes-promoter-sanjay-gupta-tata-steel-deal-stake-sale-transform-business-market-share-20039911.htm
3. Tata Steel board outcome, owning filing (Nov 22): Tubes Business (Jamshedpur, Hosur, Khopoli, Sahibabad plants + Khopoli cold-rolling/surface-coated) to APL Apollo as going-concern slump sale for up to Rs 11,700 cr cash; Tubes FY26 turnover ~Rs 11,375 cr (8.14% of Tata standalone, 4.9% consolidated), net worth ~Rs 3,274 cr (management allocation, unaudited). TSCL (Tata Steel Colors Pvt Ltd, formerly Tata BlueScope Steel) 100% sold for Rs 16,300 cr; TSCL FY26 turnover Rs 4,680 cr (3.35% standalone), net worth ~Rs 1,016 cr. BTA + SPA mutually conditional, simultaneous closure, completion proposed before March 31, 2028, subject to regulatory approvals. APL Apollo FY26: standalone turnover ~Rs 15,110 cr, consolidated ~Rs 23,079 cr (as disclosed in the filing). https://www.tatasteel.com/media/26678/bse-outcome-board-meeting.pdf
