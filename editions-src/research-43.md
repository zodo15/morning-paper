## 2. BIS closes the cloud backdoor (Monday, fresh) - second US anchor - LEGAL STATUS VERIFIED AT OWNING SOURCE
Owning sources, READ: BIS press release (Nov 16) https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology and the rule text RIN 0694-AK50 https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/rin-0694-ak50.pdf (pp1-3, 33-36 read verbatim).
- **Stage-exact, load-bearing: RELEASED, NOT YET OPERATIVE.** The IFR was "released" Nov 16 (written by/at the direction of Treasury and the NSC) and is "on display"; its own DATES block reads "This rule is effective [INSERT DATE OF PUBLICATION IN THE FEDERAL REGISTER]" and comments are due "[INSERT DATE 60 DAYS AFTER DATE OF PUBLICATION]". Federal Register search for RIN 0694-AK50 returns ZERO documents (checked Nov 17 ~4:20 AM IST) - no dated publication as of this digest. Never write that the rule is in force; verify FR publication before changing that state.
- **What it actually does (NOT a universal renting ban - keep all four limbs):**
  1. Remote-access prohibition: no export/reexport/transfer of listed ECCNs (3A001.z; 3A090 except 3A090.c; 4A003.z/4A004.z/4A005.z/4A090; 5A002.z/5A004.z/5A992.z + tied software/technology) with "knowledge" the item is destined to a data center anywhere providing >12,000,000 "aggregate TPP" (one site or linked sites) to companies headquartered in - or whose ultimate parent is headquartered in - Macau or a Country Group D:5 destination. Applies regardless of whether the holder was party to the original transaction; no de minimis reliance; KYC due-diligence expected on direct/indirect D:5/Macau ownership or control; an uncooperative data center is a red flag that presumptively confers "knowledge". BIS may also individually inform persons a license is required.
  2. Five-country license requirement: advanced-semiconductor exports/reexports/transfers to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Brazil (+ countries later designated) need a license - EXCEPT chips destined to companies headquartered in, or whose ultimate parent is headquartered in, the US, provided reporting requirements are met.
  3. Foundry/packager presumptions tightened for advanced-node logic chips destined to designers in license-required destinations.
  4. Formally rescinds the never-enforced Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule (rescission announced May 2025).
- **Conditional recommender exception:** no license where access is ONLY for a 'commercial recommender system' (e.g. online retail, social media) AND the provider files the specified disclosure at bis.gov/ai/exclusionnotesubmission (shared with the End User Review Committee).
- Compliance runway: providers of existing covered remote-access services must obtain a license within 90 days of the effective date.
