## LR50 (Long Read) — Maruti Suzuki: the people's car that built an industry (fact base, 8 sources read in full Tue 04:25 IST)

Why Maruti: never used as an LR subject; the company India's own market was built around; a holiday edition can carry a longer historical read; this week the market obsesses over NVDA/AI - a domestic manufacturing compounding story is the right counterweight.

Story spine (all dates sourced below):
1. ORIGINS (reconciled): Maruti Motors Ltd incorporated June 1971 with Sanjay Gandhi as first managing director (business-standard timeline; forbesindia dates the launch 1971 without a month). After the 1977 change of government the venture collapsed - forbesindia says the firm was 'dissolved in 1977'. The takeover then ran in two steps across sources: business-standard dates the nationalisation to 1981; forbesindia says Indira Gandhi resurrected it 'under her nationalisation drive' and that Maruti Udyog Ltd was incorporated Feb 24, 1981. EDITION WORDING (safe): 'Maruti Motors was incorporated in 1971 with Sanjay Gandhi as managing director; the venture was dissolved in 1977; after Indira Gandhi returned to power, the government took over the undertaking and incorporated Maruti Udyog Limited in February 1981.' Do not print a specific Act name/year unless a source is added. globalsuzuki (owning) says Maruti Udyog was 'formed as a state enterprise in 1981' and 'signed a car-production agreement with Suzuki in 1982' - consistent. The project was "deemed to be born in sin" - charges of abuse of public policy, irregular land allocation, money laundering against the Gandhis (business-standard).
2. SUZUKI WAS NOT FIRST CHOICE: Renault, Volkswagen, MAN AG and Ford had expressed interest; talks proceeded with Renault but the Renault 18 (1,647cc) at Rs 1.15 lakh incl. import duty killed it (business-standard). Final tech-transfer/CKD/component/JV agreements signed October 2, 1982; the partners had only 14 months to roll out a small car (business-standard). Indira handpicked leadership: Sumant Moolgaokar (Telco chairman) as non-exec chairman, V Krishnamurthy (BHEL turnaround) as vice chairman (forbesindia).
3. DEC 1983 LAUNCH: first Maruti 800, a 796cc hatchback, rolled out Dec 1983 (forbesindia); first car bought by Harpal Singh, an Indian Airlines employee (business-standard). Production began Dec 1983 (globalsuzuki 2011). When production began, the entire Indian car market was under 70,000 units/year (globalsuzuki 2011).
4. SCALE: 10 million units reached March 15, 2011 - 27 years 3 months after production start (globalsuzuki 2011 owning release). By 2010 the Indian market had passed 3 million units/year (same). Gurgaon + Manesar plants, 1.2M units/yr combined capacity in 2011 (same).
5. THE STATE EXITS: IPO 2003 subscribed 7.82x; government sold 25%, stake fell to 17%; more sold 2006; full exit 2007 (business-standard). Maruti became a subsidiary of SMC in 2002 (FY2025-26 annual report). CURRENT STAKE (owning, current): as of 31 March 2026, SMC holds 58.53% of equity - Maruti Suzuki Annual Integrated Report 2025-26, https://www.marutisuzuki.com/content/dam/arena-eds/corporate/pdf/company-reports/2025-2026/MSIL_IR_2025-26_HR_updated.pdf (also cross-checks aggregator: foreign promoter 58.72% in the Sep-2026 pattern per valueresearch citing exchange filings - use only the owning 31-Mar-2026 figure, dated). NOTE: the marutisuzuki.com 'details of the business' page still says 56.2% - it is a stale owning page (lists Alto 800/Ciaz era lineup); do NOT use its stake figure. Cross-checking revealed stake rose through the FY24 SMG share-swap era (58.19% at FY25).
6. MANESAR 2012: three labour strikes June-Oct 2011 had already rocked the new plant (business-standard). Then July 18, 2012: mob violence and arson; General Manager (HR) Awanish Kumar Dev burnt to death; nearly 100 executives/managers/supervisors hospitalised (owning Maruti statement, July 19 2012). Post-mortem: lower limbs fractured at multiple points, body charred (NYT India Ink, Jul 20 2012). More than 70 injured per NYT count; Maruti declared a lockout at the plant. Worst labour violence in Indian corporate history.
7. 800's END: after 30+ years and almost 3 million sold worldwide, Maruti 800 production ended - announced late week of Feb 7, 2014; stricter emission standards (no upgrade to BS-IV for 13 major cities) and declining sales (NYT India Ink, Feb 10 2014).
8. 20 MILLION: June 4, 2018 - Suzuki's accumulated production in India hit 20 million units, the second country after Japan, and the FASTEST: 34 years 5 months vs Japan's 45 years 9 months (globalsuzuki 2018 owning release). 20 millionth car: a Swift from the Gujarat plant (Suzuki Motor Gujarat, Suzuki's 100% subsidiary). Alto most-produced at ~3.17M. FY2017: 1.78M units produced, 1.65M sold domestic, 130k exported (same). Current scale (owning FY2025-26 report, dated to FY26): highest-ever total sales in FY2025-26, surpassing 2 million units for the third straight year - first PV maker in India to do so; country's largest PV exporter; commercial production commenced at the new Kharkhoda greenfield plant; capacity expanded at Hansalpur. marutisuzuki.com details page (stale but structural): 16 models, 150+ variants, plants Gurgaon/Manesar + R&D Rohtak.
9. THESIS for the read: from a tainted political project dismissed as doomed, to the machine that put India on four wheels and made Suzuki's India arm the parent company's emerging-market production/sales/R&D leader (business-standard) - one car every 12 seconds by 2014 (forbesindia).
