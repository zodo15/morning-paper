### FCC / SpaceX Starmind (OWNING SOURCE READ: FCC order DA 26-1273, adopted+released 25 Nov 2026, Space Bureau)
URL: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-1273A1.txt (pdf twin .pdf). Status line: "GRANTED-IN-PART/DEFERRED-IN-PART ... With Conditions". Licensee Space Exploration Holdings LLC, call sign S00798, file SAT-LOA-20260108-00016 (filed 30 Jan 2026).
- Scope of grant (verbatim): "Authority for radiofrequency communications for backup and emergency telemetry, tracking, & command (TT&C) for a constellation of 1,000,000 NGSO satellites to operate as data centers in space." It licenses the radios (Ka-band 18.8-19.3 / 28.6-29.1 GHz, unprotected, non-interference), not computing. Satellites use optical links.
- Orbits: shells of 50 km, altitudes 500-1,002 km. Milestones: first satellite by 25 Nov 2033, 10% by 25 Nov 2035 (50% by 2038, rest by 2040 per Space Law Journal/Spaceflight Now). 15-year licence term starts when first satellite certified in orbit.
- DEFERRED: waiver to dispose satellites above 600 km in disposal or heliocentric orbits (SpaceX must file a modification with final debris plan before deploying such satellites). Nothing is deployed yet.
- Safe copy: "The FCC's Space Bureau granted SpaceX a licence, in part, for up to one million Starmind data-centre satellites. The grant covers the satellites' backup radio links; disposal plans for anything above 600 km are deferred, and the first satellite must launch by November 2033." Do NOT say "approved a million data centres in orbit" or "cleared to launch a million".
- Corroboration: CNBC 25 Nov https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-fcc-satellite-approval.html (Carr announcement, Amazon/Viasat/AAS petitions), Spaceflight Now https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/26/fcc-green-lights-spacexs-plan-to-launch-1-million-starmind-data-center-satellites/ , Space Law Journal https://spacelawjournal.com/fcc-spacex-starmind-million-satellite-licence/ .
