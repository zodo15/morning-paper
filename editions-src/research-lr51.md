# LR51 research: Dixon Technologies - the factory behind the brands
Compiled 04:20-04:35 IST, 25 Nov 2026. Editorial rationale: never covered (nearest: LR15 Zetwerk, industrial contract manufacturing - different sector); resonates with today's Tata Steel/SG Tech colour-coated contract-manufacturing MOU; strong owning-source trail (DRHP, company site, Counterpoint, MeitY-linked milestones).

## The arc (all facts sourced below)
1. Founded Jan 15, 1993 at Alwar, Rajasthan as "Weston Utilities Limited" - the father's company name inside the son's. Renamed Dixon Utilities & Exports July 1993; Dixon Technologies (India) Pvt Ltd Jan 2006; public May 2, 2017. (DRHP, SEBI)
2. Sunil Vachani, 23, back from American College London; cordless-phone factory 1989-90 failed; trained at father Sundar Vachani's Weston (TVs; hit hard by USSR collapse). First Dixon factory: 10,000 sq ft rented Noida plot, a few machines, 15 employees, Rs 15 lakh borrowed from his father. Atul Lall, Weston's "blue-eyed boy" (joined 1986), sent as mentor - became co-founder/vice-chairman. (Forbes 2022/2024)
3. First order: Lucky-Goldstar (pre-LG, then only a rep office) - a few thousand export TVs at $1.5 per TV fee: "we were so desperate for business." When LG entered India, Dixon made its domestic TVs. Then Philips (durables/DVD/audio/VCR), SEGA consoles. Profitable from the first quarter; first-year profit Rs 3 lakh. (Forbes 2024)
4. CRT-only for 8-9 years; 1997 export business shock taught concentration risk. Early-2000s own-brand detour, abandoned after ~2.5 years: "sometimes the decisions of what not to do are far more important." The vow, "cast in stone": never compete with customers. (Forbes 2022, Vachani's words)
5. Bureaucrats in the early 2000s: "India's future does not lie in electronics hardware, it's software." Funding rejections; one bank lent against secured exports. One loss-making year ever - an unhedged INR-USD move (Vachani via Forbes).
6. May 2008: Motilal Oswal PE invests at sub-Rs 150 cr valuation; drills lowest-cost-producer, ROCE, working capital, backward integration. 2008: CFL lighting; washing machines for Godrej follow. (Forbes 2022, Tulsyan quotes)
7. Sept 2017 IPO (listing approvals Sept 15, effective listing Sept 18, 2017 per IBEF) - trigger: exit for Motilal Oswal. Forbes (2022): split-adjusted listing price Rs 352, ~10x to Rs 3,546 at writing (2022). Vachani's claim it was among the most-subscribed EMS IPOs - his words, keep framed as such or drop.
8. DRHP economics: revenue Rs 767 cr (FY13) -> Rs 1,389 cr (FY16) -> Rs 1,845 cr (9M FY17); revenue CAGR FY13-16 34.6%. FY16 EMS/ODM production shares (Frost & Sullivan, in DRHP): FPD TVs 50.4%, washing machines 42.6%, LED/CFL lights 38.9%.
9. Mobile pivot: Padget Electronics JV makes phones from 2016; Nokia/HMD contract 2021; PLI era (2020) - Dixon in five PLI schemes (mobiles, IT hardware, telecom, LED components, AC components). Padget Noida: motherboard floor runs 24x7 automated, 40,000 units/day (Forbes 2022 floor visit).
10. Scale now: #1 Indian EMS (company site), $5.18B operating revenue, 30 manufacturing facilities, 7 R&D centres, 100+ global brands. Xiaomi smart TVs from 2018, then smartphones (Xiaomi India president Muralikrishnan B quote, Forbes 2024).
11. Counterpoint (Apr 2026, owning): Dixon led India's 2025 smartphone EMS rankings, +89% YoY production on Motorola/realme/Xiaomi orders; per IBEF milestone table, Q2 CY25 Dixon became India's largest smartphone maker by shipments (+196% YoY, >22% share, surpassing Samsung). 'Made in India' smartphones +8% YoY 2025, exports +28% (~1/3 of output); electronics third-largest export category FY25, tracking second in FY26.
12. The margin mirror: Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026, ET citing exchange filing): revenue Rs 21,794 cr (+46.7% YoY); net profit Rs 343.7 cr (-48.7%); EBITDA Rs 666 cr (-37%); margin 3.1% vs 7.1%. H1 FY27: revenue Rs 37,938 cr (+35%), PAT Rs 1,007 cr (+13%). Stock +11% to Rs 12,199 intraday on results day. (FreePress/consol PAT Rs 411 cr -45% is the other measure - if a single PAT figure is printed, use ET's exchange-filing-linked Rs 343.7 cr net profit.)
13. The next integration: MeitY-approved JV with Longcheer (Dixon 74%, Longcheer 26% via Singapore arm) for smartphones/tablets/wearables/AI PCs/auto/healthcare electronics; FY26 capex Rs 1,100-1,200 cr (displays, camera modules, optical transceivers, enclosures); Signify OEM lighting JV (Jun 2025, completing ~Nov 30, 2025); Rs 1,000 cr Tamil Nadu laptop/AIO facility (Oragadam, ~5,000 jobs). (IBEF)
14. Voice: "microscopic and telescopic vision at the same time" (father's advice); "I am a paranoid entrepreneur... if we don't change with times, we might just be disrupted"; consensus-building over dictat (Lall on Vachani). 2022: Forbes billionaire ($1.1bn).
